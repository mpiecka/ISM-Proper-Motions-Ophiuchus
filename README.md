# ISM-Proper-Motions-Ophiuchus
Visualisation of the proper motions investigated in the Ophiuchus/L1688 region. Based on images obtained by HST (epoch 2016, program ID: 14181) and JWST (epoch 2023, program ID: DD 2739).

The 3D visualisation HURACAN_Oph_3dmap.html was generated using plotly and based on: CO spectra obtained by White et al. 2015 (https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stu2323), the Gaia DR3-based L1688 stellar population from Ratzenböck et al. 2023 (https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243690), HD 147889 and Oph S1 radial velocities from Gontcharov 2012 (https://doi.org/10.1134/S1063773712120031) and Gaia DR3, respectively, and dust map by Leike et al. 2020 (https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202038169).

Submitted to A&A.
